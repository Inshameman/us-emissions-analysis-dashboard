# us-emissions-analysis-dashboard
Interactive US CO₂ Emissions Analysis Dashboard built using Databricks. Explores state-wise and county-wise emissions, population trends, emissions per person, and top emission-contributing counties through data visualization.

United States Emissions Breakdown (2023 EPA Data)

An interactive analytics dashboard built in Databricks to explore U.S. greenhouse gas/emissions patterns across geographic regions, population density, and local jurisdictions using Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) data from 2023.

Key Visualizations & Insights:

Geographic Distribution (Continental U.S. Map): Visualizes emission coverage across the contiguous United States.

State Concentration (Donut Chart): Breaks down share of total U.S. emissions across the top 10 states (TX, FL, OH, IL, GA, MO, CA, TN, PA, NC), which together account for 51% of total emissions—led by TX (20.63%), FL (16.47%), and OH (9.71%).

Emissions vs. Population (Scatter Plot): Compares population against emissions per person, illustrating the trend that higher-population areas tend to have lower emissions per capita.

Top Emitting Counties (Horizontal Bar Chart): Ranks the highest-emitting counties by total metric tons of $\text{CO}_2$, led by Maricopa County, AZ (9.81M), Harris County, TX (9.68M), and Cook County, IL (8.11M).

Tools & Stack:
Platform: Databricks (Dashboards / Lakehouse)

Data Source: U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) — 2023 dataset

Mapping: Mapbox / OpenStreetMap
